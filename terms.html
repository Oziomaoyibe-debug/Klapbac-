Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Klapbac collects and uses your personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Who is responsible for your data

Klapbac (operated from Nigeria) is the data controller. Contact and data protection enquiries: klapbac@proton.me.

What we collect

  • Account details: email address, username, school, and your password (stored in protected form by our login provider; we cannot read it).
  • Your activity: posts, comments, likes, blocks, reports you send, and your alias setting.
  • Technical data: device type, browser, IP address and security logs kept by our hosting providers.
  • Stored in your browser: a login session token, so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics.

We do not ask for sensitive information such as health, religion or political opinions. If you choose to post such details, they become part of your public content.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To create your account and run the service: your consent and to provide what you asked for.
  • To keep the platform safe, handle reports and prevent abuse: our legitimate interest and legal obligations.
  • To improve Klapbac: our legitimate interest, using only basic usage data.

We do not sell your data and we do not make decisions about you using automated profiling.

Who can see what

  • Other users see your username and school on normal posts, or your alias on anonymous posts.
  • Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • Reports are visible only to the Klapbac team.
  • Anonymous posts are linked to your account in our systems. We reveal that link only when the law requires it, for example under a valid court order, or to prevent serious harm.

Who we share data with

Service providers that process data for us: Supabase (database and login), Vercel (hosting) and Google Fonts (fonts). We may also share data with the police, courts or regulators where the law requires it.

Transfers outside Nigeria

Our providers may store or process data in other countries. We choose providers that apply appropriate safeguards, and we transfer data only as the Nigeria Data Protection Act allows.

How long we keep your data

We keep your account data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data and posts within a reasonable time, except where the law requires us to keep certain records (for example, reports about serious abuse).

Your rights

You may ask us to: give you a copy of your data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to how we use it; give you your data in a portable form; and withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect what we did before). Contact klapbac@proton.me. We will reply within a reasonable time. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Security and data breaches

We use encryption in transit, access controls and row-level security in our database. No system is completely secure. If a breach is likely to harm you, we will tell the NDPC and you as the law requires.

Children and under-18s

Under Nigerian law a child is a person under 18, and a child's data may be processed only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. Klapbac is open to people aged 16 and over. If you are 16 or 17, you must have your parent or guardian's consent to use Klapbac, and we may ask you to confirm it. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 16 to use Klapbac, and we will delete an account if we learn the user is under 16, or if a parent or guardian asks us to remove a minor's account.
